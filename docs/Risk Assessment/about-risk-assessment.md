Risk Assessment is a digital platform for managing information on the risks your company is exposed to, based on questionnaires. Risk Assessment lets you collect specific datasets and define custom checklists for use throughout the company. This makes it easy to conduct internal audits for compliance with customs processes or AEO criteria, or to collect information on critical end-use by your customers.

Risk Assessment questionnaires can conveniently be integrated into your business processes, thanks to the integration in Export Control checks.
