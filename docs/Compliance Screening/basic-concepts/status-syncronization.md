This section is relevant to you, if you are planning to handle matches directly in your partner system instead of AEB Compliance Screening Web Application.

AEBs Compliance Screening allows you to screen your business partners against restricted party lists directly from a partner system (such as ERP etc.). The relevant API calls will respond with information on whether a business partner has been found on one of those lists, but you are required to act upon this information (see ).
