A *jurisdiction* is an area, such as a country, for which you wish to define distinct rules for export control checks.  Jurisdictions in combination with manual restrictions allow you to customize the rules that are applied during checks.

Typical export control jurisdictions are EU Dual Use, US EAR, or GB export regulations.
Export Controls comes with some predefined jurisdictions.
